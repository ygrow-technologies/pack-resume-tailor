Task: Extract hard skills and soft skills from the given job description.

Input:

* Job Description: provided by the application.

Rules:

* Output valid JSON only.
* Use the exact JSON keys `hard_skills` and `soft_skills`.
* Do not include markdown, explanation, comments, or text outside the JSON.
* Extract only skills, technologies, tools, platforms, methods, domains, and work-style signals that are explicitly present or clearly implied by the job description.
* Use the exact wording from the job description whenever possible.
* Place programming languages, frameworks, libraries, databases, cloud platforms, infrastructure, APIs, AI/ML concepts, architecture patterns, tools, and technical domains under `hard_skills`.
* Place collaboration, leadership, communication, ownership, mentoring, prioritization, stakeholder management, product thinking, adaptability, execution quality, problem solving, and similar interpersonal signals under `soft_skills`.
* Do not add unrelated skills.
* Do not duplicate near-identical skills.
* Keep each skill concise and ATS-friendly.

Validation before final output:

* Confirm the JSON keys are exactly `hard_skills` and `soft_skills`.
* Confirm every extracted item is grounded in the job description.
* Confirm hard skills and soft skills are separated correctly.
* Confirm no extra text appears outside the JSON.

Output format:
{
  "hard_skills": ["Python", "FastAPI", "PostgreSQL", "AWS"],
  "soft_skills": ["Collaboration", "Ownership", "Communication"]
}
